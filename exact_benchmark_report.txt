Key Research Insights:
1. Tree Ensembles vs. Deep Learning: The Proposed Triple Voting Ensemble and Random Forest outperform basic
Deep Learning models on single-channel sEMG due to high non-linear partition capability on Hjorth complexity
and Spectral Entropy features.
2. Representation Learning: The 1D-CNN Autoencoder compresses 1500-sample raw windows into latent
bottleneck embeddings, effectively filtering high-frequency noise and mains hum.
